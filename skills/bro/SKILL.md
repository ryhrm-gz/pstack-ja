Read [the language policy](../../references/japanese-language-policy.md) before writing or delegating. It applies to direct invocation as well as calls from `poteto-mode`.

Restate your last message. Remove unnecessary jargon and speak coherently. Keep established technical terms when they are more precise, explaining them briefly on first use. State it simply and concisely, like one human talking to another. Preserve qualifications and the connections between sentences; do not turn the explanation into disconnected fragments.
